Dear PhAST Hiring Team,

PhAST's mission --- using AI and single-cell imaging to make antibiotic susceptibility testing fast, precise, and accessible --- is the kind of problem I want to spend my career on. I am writing to apply for a machine learning / software engineering role at your Zurich office, where my background in ML, computer vision, and high-performance C++ aligns closely with what the team is building.

Over the past eight years I have worked at the intersection of machine learning and high-performance systems. At Luminocity and before that at Mindcraft, I built and maintained real-time rendering engines: GPU pipelines, particle systems, and data streaming for large-scale 3D datasets in the browser. That work taught me how to make heavy computational workloads fast and reliable --- the same discipline required to process and analyze high-throughput single-cell imaging data at scale. Before that, at the Institute of Computational Science, I contributed to Utopia, a high-performance C++ parallel computing library for non-linear high-dimensional problems, and I hold a Master's in Computational Science with coursework in deep learning, stochastic optimization, and numerical analysis.

Two projects of mine are directly relevant to PhAST's domain. First, I developed a browser-based 3D DICOM medical imaging viewer: volumetric rendering, GPU-accelerated visualization, and DICOM parsing, built with privacy in mind so that all processing happens client-side. I understand both the technical and the sensitivity constraints of medical imaging. Second, I implemented a spectral segmentation algorithm in a Blender add-on that automatically detects 3D object features --- a computer vision result that received 200+ downloads and positive feedback from the community. I also applied machine learning to 3D object classification at Hegias, where I helped the company grow into one of Switzerland's top-10 startups to watch.

On the infrastructure side, I am comfortable owning the full delivery path: AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, and Linux deployments, with Python, C++, and Rust as my main languages, and PyTorch, CUDA, and OpenMPI when the workload demands it. In a small, interdisciplinary team like PhAST's, I expect to contribute beyond a single layer --- from model and data pipelines to the platform that clinicians and pharma partners actually use.

Living in Switzerland, I would be glad to join the Zurich team in person, and I am equally happy to discuss how my profile could fit other roles you are hiring for, including research-oriented positions. I would welcome the chance to talk about how I can contribute to PhAST's mission.
